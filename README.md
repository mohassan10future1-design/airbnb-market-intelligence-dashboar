
Guest Experience Analytics Platform
Analysis of 279,712 listings and 5,373,143 reviews across 10 cities, using SQL and Power BI.
Dashboard Pages
1.	Market Overview: listings by city, property type, and room type
2.	Pricing and Property: median and average price by city (local currency)
3.	Host Performance: hosts, Superhost share, response and acceptance rates
4.	Review Activity: reviews by city, ratings, and yearly trend
Key Insights
•	Paris is the largest market (64,690 listings)
•	Entire places are about 65% of listings
•	Median price is a more reliable measure than average (outliers)
•	Review activity peaked around 2019 and then dropped

Validation
All key dashboard figures were matched against SQL.
